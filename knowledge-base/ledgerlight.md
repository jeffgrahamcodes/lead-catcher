## What to bring to a tax prep appointment

- A photo ID

- W-2s, 1099s and any other income forms
- Mortgage interest statement (Form 1098) and property tax bills

You can bring paper documents or upload them to our secure client portal before the meeting.

## Policies

- New clients: we're accepting new individual and small business clients. During tax season (January 15 to April 15), new client spots fill up, so we recommend booking early.
- Cancellations: please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead. Missed paid appointments are charged in full.
- Payment: we accept credit cards, debit cards and ACH bank transfer. Tax prep fees are due when the return is ready to file.
- Extensions: we can file a free extension for existing clients if documents arrive after April 1.
- Privacy: we never ask for Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or passwords over the phone or by email. Sensitive documents go through the secure client portal.

## Frequently asked questions

**1. How much will my tax return cost?**
It depends on your situation. A simple W-2 return with one state starts at $250. Self-employed returns start at $650. We confirm the exact price after reviewing your documents, before any work begins.

**2. Do you offer free consultations?**
Yes. The 15-minute intro call is free and is the best way to find out if we're a good fit.

**3. Can I meet virtually instead of coming in?**
Yes. Every appointment except the intro call can be in our office or by video. The intro call is by phone.

**4. How long does it take to get my return done?**
Most individual returns are ready within 7 to 10 business days after we have all your documents. During March and April it can take up to 3 weeks.

**5. I got a letter from the IRS. What should I do?**
Don't ignore it, and don't panic. Most notices are routine. Book an IRS notice review and bring the letter. Many notices have a response deadline, so book soon.

**6. Do you work with freelancers and gig workers?**
Yes. A large share of our clients are freelancers, contractors and rideshare or delivery drivers. We help with quarterly estimated taxes too.

**7. I haven't filed in a few years. Can you help?**
Yes. We prepare prior-year and late returns starting at $300 per year, and we can help set up a payment plan if you owe.

**8. Do you serve Maryland and Virginia?**
Yes. We file DC, Maryland and Virginia returns, plus returns for most other states.

**9. Can you do my bookkeeping too?**
Yes. Monthly bookkeeping starts at $300 per month. Book a free bookkeeping onboarding call to get started.

**10. Are you open on weekends?**
We're open Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and closed Sundays. Our AI assistant takes calls and books appointments any time.

## Rules for the AI assistant

- Never give tax advice or say what someone owes or will get back. Offer an appointment with a CPA instead.
- Only quote "starting at" prices from this document. Never promise a final price.
- Never ask for a Social Security number, bank details or passwords.
- Only offer appointment times that the scheduling tool returns.
- If a caller mentions an IRS notice with a deadline in the next 7 days, mark the lead as urgent so the owner is notified.
- If a caller asks for a person, take their name, phone number and the reason for the call, and promise a callback the next business day.
- If a question isn't answered here, say a team member will follow up. Don't guess.
